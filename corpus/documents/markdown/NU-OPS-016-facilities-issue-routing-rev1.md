# NU-OPS-016 — Facilities Issue Routing, Revision 1

**Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC)**  
**Status:** Draft historical copy (planned status: superseded) · **Revision:** 1  
**Proposed issue date:** 25 September 2023 · **Proposed effective period:** 1 October 2023–20 September 2024  
**Proposed replacement:** NU-OPS-003, if approved, effective 21 September 2024 · **Access scope:** `site_operations`

> Fictional educational test material. This proposed historical procedure describes non-clinical facilities routing only. It is not a current instruction and does not provide clinical, emergency-response, or patient-care guidance.

## 1. Purpose and applicability

This draft preserves a proposed earlier route for ordinary facilities and workspace issues at Harbor Point, Alder Creek, and Northgate. Its dates and process are invented for historical retrieval testing. The proposal is not evidence that this version was approved or used.

The proposed scope is building and shared-workspace conditions such as a damaged fixture, lighting fault, leak, or facilities repair request. It does not determine clinical operations or establish how to respond to a clinical or emergency condition.

## 2. Proposed prior issue categories

For the proposed prior process, site staff used two local labels:

| Local label | Proposed meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Follow-up | A repair or workspace request that could be logged for the site lead’s next routine review. |
| Needs site attention | A condition for which the site lead should be notified during the current site handoff. |

These labels are historical proposals only. They do not map to the categories proposed in NU-OPS-003 and should not be used to classify a current issue.

## 3. Proposed former routing path

Under this proposed Revision 1 path, the staff member who noticed the issue entered it in the local site facilities log or gave the details to the site lead for entry. The site lead reviewed the log, recorded the local priority label, and assigned a local follow-up owner when one was available.

This proposed route used the **site lead and local site log as the normal intake and tracking point**. It did not use a central Facilities Coordinator queue or require a routine central facilities ticket. For “Needs site attention,” the site lead was notified through the site’s ordinary handoff channel. No shared central mailbox, response time, or cross-site escalation route is established by this proposed historical copy.

The site log entry was proposed to contain the site, area, date observed, short description, local label, assigned role if known, and a brief status note. The site lead reviewed open entries at the next local handoff and marked the entry resolved when the site recorded a completed action or a documented transfer to another process.

## 4. Proposed former escalation and follow-up

If the site lead could not identify a local owner, the proposed practice was to leave the item open in the site log and raise it at the next routine operations coordination meeting. This copy does not establish who attended that meeting, a meeting schedule, a central intake owner, or a guaranteed response.

If a condition also required another reporting route, the local log could note that a separate report had been made. This proposed copy does not define that other process or authorize copying sensitive details into the facilities log.
