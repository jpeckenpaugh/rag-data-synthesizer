# Site Opening and Closing Checklist

**NU-OPS-002 · Current**\
Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative · All sites  
Version 1.0 · Effective 2024-09-21 · Access scope: `site_operations`

> Fictional educational test material. This checklist covers routine, non-clinical site handoffs at Harbor Point, Alder Creek, and Northgate. It is not operational guidance for a real organization.

## Applicability and roles

Use this checklist for the routine opening and closing handoff at each NUCC site. The site operations staff member completing a handoff records the result. The local site lead coordinates unresolved facility or access issues and confirms the next owner when follow-up is needed.

Tessa Quill, Harbor Point Site Lead, coordinates this cross-site checklist. The site lead at each location is responsible for local use and for identifying a site-specific conflict. This checklist does not grant one site lead authority to change another site's local arrangements or an organization-wide requirement.

Complete the listed checks only within the staff member’s assigned role and access. This document covers facilities, workspace, and administrative handoff status. It does not set care-readiness or clinical opening criteria.

## Before opening

Record the date, site, scheduled opening handoff time, and completing role in the site’s opening record. Use role labels rather than personal contact details in shared notes.

- Confirm that the work area and shared administrative spaces are in their expected condition for the site’s routine opening.
- Check visible, non-clinical facility conditions that affect use of the workspace, including obvious access obstruction, lighting, water intrusion, or equipment damage. Do not attempt an unassigned repair.
- Confirm that required shared keys or access items are present in the site’s designated handoff location. Record a missing item; do not borrow or duplicate an item outside the site’s assigned process.
- Note any open facilities issue by its issue record ID and current status, if known. Do not mark a prior issue resolved unless the responsible owner has confirmed closure.
- If a condition prevents the ordinary site handoff, notify the local site lead and route the facilities issue through the Facilities Coordinator using NU-OPS-007 and the applicable facilities procedure.

## Opening handoff and readiness record

**Approved local recording convention.** Use the following outcome labels in the site record for routine facilities and workspace handoffs:

| Result | Use when |
| --- | --- |
| Complete | Listed non-clinical checks were completed and no unresolved condition prevents the routine workspace handoff. |
| Complete with open item | The routine handoff can proceed, but a condition has been recorded for follow-up. Include the issue record ID and next owner if known. |
| Hold for site lead | The handoff cannot be recorded as complete because a listed check is unresolved or the site lead must coordinate next steps. State the observed condition and time; do not speculate about its cause. |

**Follow-up for a “Hold for site lead” entry:** the local site lead records the coordination outcome or assigned follow-up owner. A checklist entry records observed status; it is not approval to change a separate procedure.

## During-day issue logging

If a new facilities or workspace condition is noticed after opening, record the site, observation time, brief factual description, and issue record ID when available. Route the item under the applicable facilities procedure. For time-sensitive site impact, notify the local site lead and the central role specified in NU-OPS-007.

Update the handoff record when responsibility changes or the owner confirms closure. If the destination is unclear, use the Operations Desk Coordinator route listed in NU-OPS-007 to resolve the routing question. Do not invent a backup contact or response time.

## Closing handoff

Before the closing handoff is complete, the assigned staff member:

- Records the site and handoff time.
- Notes the status of open facilities items, using issue record IDs where available.
- Leaves shared administrative work areas in the site’s ordinary orderly condition and returns shared access items to their designated location.
- Records any missing access item, newly observed damage, or condition requiring follow-up, without assigning an unconfirmed cause.
- Routes new facilities issues to the Facilities Coordinator and informs the local site lead when local coordination is needed.
- Identifies the role receiving the next handoff, or records that the next owner is not yet confirmed.

The closing staff member does not close an issue record solely because the site is closing. The issue remains open until its assigned owner confirms disposition under the applicable procedure.

## After-hours access boundary

Routine opening and closing steps apply at all three sites. Any Harbor Point after-hours access exception is controlled only by NU-OPS-004 for the site, roles, subject, and dates stated in that notice. This checklist does not establish the exception’s access window or eligibility and does not extend it to Alder Creek, Northgate, another date, or another activity. All unrelated checklist and handoff requirements remain in effect.

## Escalation and record retention

Use NU-OPS-007 as the current fictional role-based directory. Use the applicable facilities procedure for issue fields, priority, and follow-up. This checklist does not create a response-time commitment or a separate emergency route.

Retain the completed opening and closing records in the site’s designated operations record location under the local site lead’s coordination. If that location or its retention period is not established for a site, ask the site lead to confirm it; do not store the record in an assumed location.
