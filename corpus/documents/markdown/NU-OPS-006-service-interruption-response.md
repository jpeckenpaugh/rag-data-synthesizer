# Service Interruption Response

**NU-OPS-006 · Draft for corpus review**  
Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative · All sites  
Version 1.0 · Effective 2024-10-01 (proposed) · Access scope: `site_operations`

> Fictional educational test material. This procedure addresses non-clinical operational coordination for the fictional Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC). It is not guidance for a real organization and does not define clinical response.

## Purpose and scope

Use this procedure when an unplanned condition interrupts or materially limits a non-clinical site or shared administrative function. Examples include loss of ordinary building access, a facility condition that limits use of workspace, or an outage of a shared administrative system or communication channel.

This procedure coordinates status, ownership, and updates. It does not determine whether care can be provided, set reopening criteria, or replace the applicable facilities, workplace incident, severe-weather, or other process. Do not use this procedure to infer a clinical response or an emergency contact route.

## Identify and record the interruption

The staff member who first identifies the condition records the facts available at that time and notifies the local site lead. Do not delay a notification to establish the cause. Record at minimum:

- Site or shared function affected.
- What is observed and when it was first noticed, if known.
- Current status and the time that status was last confirmed.
- Actions already taken and any known operational limitation.
- The role receiving the next update, if assigned.
- Related record IDs, if the condition also requires a separate facilities or incident record.

Use the applicable procedure for additional required fields. Do not include personal or sensitive details in a general operations update.

## Coordination and routing

The local site lead coordinates the site’s initial status and handoff. Notify the Director of Operations for cross-site coordination using the role-based route in NU-OPS-007. When a facilities condition is involved, also route it to the Facilities Coordinator under NU-OPS-003. NU-OPS-007 identifies the fictional destinations; it does not promise a response time or restoration.

If the interruption affects more than one site or a shared function, the Director of Operations coordinates the cross-site summary and identifies a next update owner. Each affected site lead remains responsible for reporting that site’s observed status. A staff member should not announce a network-wide status based only on one site’s report.

If a listed route is unavailable, follow the after-hours path in NU-OPS-007 and record the attempted route and time in the applicable record. If no applicable destination is established, record the routing gap and ask the Operations Desk Coordinator to confirm it; do not invent an alternate contact.

## Status and update convention

**Proposed status labels — pending coordinator approval.** The labels below are suggested for consistent administrative updates. They are not an approved organization-wide status vocabulary until the coordinator accepts them.

These labels are for general service interruptions. For severe-weather site updates, use the status fields and labels in NU-OPS-013; do not substitute this table’s labels.

| Proposed label | Meaning for the update |
| --- | --- |
| Monitoring | A condition has been reported; current impact or next action is still being confirmed. |
| Limited | A site or function remains available with a stated non-clinical limitation. Describe only the observed limitation. |
| Interrupted | The affected site or function is not available for its ordinary non-clinical operation, based on the latest confirmation. |
| Restored, pending confirmation | A reporter has observed a return of the affected function, but the responsible owner has not yet confirmed closure of related work. |
| Closed | The coordination owner has recorded the final status, outstanding follow-up owner if any, and closure time. |

**Proposed update cadence — pending coordinator approval.** While an interruption remains active, the assigned update owner should provide a status update every 60 minutes, and sooner when a material status change occurs. If that interval cannot be met, record the reason and next expected update time. This proposed cadence is a coordination target only; it is not a response or restoration commitment.

For severe-weather site updates, follow NU-OPS-013’s material-change and shift-handoff update convention. NU-OPS-013 establishes no fixed timed cadence, so the proposed 60-minute interval above does not apply to its site-status updates.

Each update should state the affected site or function, status label (if approved), time last confirmed, known actions, unresolved limitation, next owner, and next update time. Separate confirmed observations from estimates or unverified reports. Do not state a cause, impact, or recovery time as fact unless confirmed by the responsible role.

## Related records: facilities and workplace incidents

An operational interruption may require more than one record. If a facilities issue contributes to the interruption, create or update the facilities issue record under NU-OPS-003 and include its ID in the interruption summary. The facilities record remains the source for facilities intake, priority, and work follow-up; this procedure does not replace it.

If the event also meets the scope of a non-clinical workplace incident, make or update the separate report under NU-OPS-011. Link the incident ID to the interruption summary where appropriate, while keeping each record’s purpose distinct. A service interruption entry is not a substitute for an incident report, and an incident report does not by itself establish operational restoration.

## Severe weather handoff

For a severe-weather coordination event, use NU-OPS-013 for its site-status and communication steps, and use this procedure for any broader service-interruption coordination that remains applicable. Do not invent a forecast, reopening time, closure threshold, or weather-specific decision rule. Report only the site status confirmed by the responsible site role and follow the established handoff route.

## Recovery, closure, and handoff

The local site lead reports when a site or function appears restored and provides the time last confirmed. The Director of Operations records the cross-site coordination status when multiple sites or a shared function were affected. The responsible owner confirms the disposition of any linked facilities or incident records under their respective procedures.

Do not treat “restored” as proof that every linked issue is closed. Keep unresolved follow-up assigned to a role and identify the next update owner. The interruption may be marked closed only after the coordination owner records the final confirmed status, closure time, and any remaining follow-up owner. If a closure condition is not established, leave the status open and state what remains unknown.

## Approval and revision record

Under NU-OPS-001, this cross-site procedure requires approval by the Director of Operations before it becomes an approved instruction. Until approval is recorded in the controlled copy, the proposed labels, cadence, and process details in this draft are not operative requirements.
