# Harbor Point After-Hours Access Exception

**NU-OPS-004 · Approved and scheduled; inactive as of 2024-10-01**\
Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative · Harbor Point only  
Version 1.0 · Approved window: 2024-10-07 through 2024-10-11 · Access scope: `operations_leads`

> Fictional educational test material. This approved exception concerns a limited non-clinical administrative task at the fictional Harbor Point site. It is not access or security guidance for a real organization.

## Purpose and approval status

This approved notice establishes a narrow exception to the after-hours access boundary in NU-OPS-002 for a scheduled administrative storage-shelf inventory and relabeling task at Harbor Point. It does not change any opening, closing, facilities, or other site requirement.

The Director of Operations approved this exception on 2024-09-30. It is scheduled to take effect on 2024-10-07, so it is inactive as of the fixed corpus snapshot date, 2024-10-01. It may be used only during its stated window and only while the approved base checklist is effective.

## Site, roles, task, and dates

During its approved window, the exception applies only:

- **Site:** Harbor Point.
- **Task:** Inventory and relabel shelves in the designated non-clinical administrative supply storage area. No other work is included.
- **Eligible role:** Harbor Point Site Lead only, when performing this named task. The Director of Operations approved this exception; approval does not make another role eligible to perform the task under it.
- **Days:** Monday through Friday, 2024-10-07 through 2024-10-11 inclusive.
- **Access window:** 18:00 to 19:30 local site time on each listed day.

The window does not authorize early entry, access after 19:30, weekend access, or access by another role or visitor. It does not extend to Alder Creek or Northgate, another area or activity, or another date.

## Check-in and record

For each use of the exception, the Harbor Point Site Lead records the date, arrival and departure times, role present, task performed, and any incomplete work or issue record ID in the Harbor Point site operations handoff record. Use role names and do not add unnecessary personal details. No other role is eligible to perform the exception task.

If the task cannot be completed within the stated window, stop the exception work at 19:30, record what remains, and route any facilities issue through the applicable procedure and NU-OPS-007. A facilities route does not grant task or retrieval eligibility to the Facilities Coordinator under this exception. This notice does not authorize an extension or create an alternate routing path.

## Requirements that remain in force

All requirements in NU-OPS-002 remain in force except the limited after-hours access boundary for the eligible roles, task, site, and dates stated above. In particular:

- The ordinary opening and closing handoff and recordkeeping requirements are unchanged.
- No other activity, work area, role, visitor, or site is covered by this exception.
- Facilities issues must still be routed and tracked under the applicable facilities procedure.
- This exception does not change access-item handling or authorize unassigned repair work.
- A checklist entry does not approve a separate procedure or extend this notice.

## Expiration and review

This exception expires automatically at 19:30 local site time on 2024-10-11. It does not renew automatically. Any later or extended access requires a separately approved notice that identifies its own scope and dates. Before 2024-10-07, and after expiration, the base procedure applies without this exception. The approved exception does not authorize activity outside its stated window.

The Harbor Point Site Lead records whether the task was completed and any remaining follow-up. That completion record does not extend the access window.
