> Fiction notice: NUCC, its employees, schedules, roles, and contact routes are invented for an educational RAG test corpus. This notice covers administrative scheduling records only. It does not provide clinical staffing or care guidance.

## Notice purpose and effective date

This notice establishes the administrative path for requesting, approving, and recording a change to a published staff schedule at Harbor Point, Alder Creek, or Northgate. Effective **2024-09-21**, it supersedes NU-OPS-017 for requests handled on or after that date. NU-OPS-017 remains available for historical questions about its prior period.

## What counts as a schedule change

The scope is a request to change an already published work date, shift assignment, or site assignment. A request to correct a clerical error is identified as a correction so the record owner can distinguish it from a requested change.

This notice does not define minimum coverage, qualifications, clinical assignments, or whether a proposed schedule is operationally adequate. It records an administrative request and its disposition; it does not determine clinical or site readiness.

## Approval roles

The **site lead for the affected site** approves a schedule change that affects only that site. A request affecting more than one site, a central-office schedule, or the schedule of the site lead is routed to the **Director of Operations** for a decision. The People Operations Coordinator maintains the administrative record but does not approve a request solely by receiving or recording it. These approver assignments are the approved current rule; NU-OPS-017 describes the prior rule for historical questions.

Under this rule, a decision must be explicit and attributable to the applicable approver role. A submitted request, a copied email, a draft calendar entry, or silence does not constitute approval. If the applicable approver is unavailable, or the request does not fit the cases above, no delegate is inferred and the request remains pending while the People Operations Coordinator clarifies the route.

## How to submit and record a change

Use the following intake fields and record workflow:

1. The requester sends the request to the site lead for the affected site. If it spans sites, concerns a central-office schedule, or affects the site lead’s own schedule, it goes to the Director of Operations through the role-based route in NU-OPS-007.
2. The request fields are affected site or sites, date or dates, the published assignment being changed, the requested change, and the requester’s role. Omit unrelated personal details.
3. The approver records one of these dispositions: **approved**, **declined**, or **pending**. An approved record identifies the approving role and decision date. A declined record states the disposition without unnecessary explanation. A pending record identifies the next role responsible for follow-up when known.
4. The recipients of the disposition are the requester and People Operations Coordinator. The Coordinator updates the administrative schedule-change log after receiving an explicit decision and retains the request with its disposition.
5. The handoff uses the recorded disposition. A request alone does not change the published record.

The role-based mailbox for process questions and schedule-change routing is `people-operations@northstar-uc.example.invalid`, as listed in the current NU-OPS-007 directory. The mailbox is fictional corpus data; this notice does not promise response times or establish a substitute approver.

## Same-day change routing

The same-day path is to mark the subject **same-day schedule request** and contact the affected site lead using the route in NU-OPS-007, in addition to sending the written request to the applicable approver. If the case falls under the Director of Operations decision route, that role’s route is also used. The request remains pending until an explicit decision is received and entered. This notice does not define an emergency or clinical coverage process.

Neither the contact route nor the Coordinator’s recordkeeping role grants approval authority.
