# Staff Schedule Change Notice

**Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC)**  
**Document ID:** NU-OPS-005 · **Status:** Draft (planned status: current) · **Version:** 2.0  
**Proposed issue date:** 2024-09-15 · **Proposed effective date:** 2024-09-21 · **Proposed review date:** 2025-09-21  
**Scope:** Organization-wide · **Access scope:** `all_staff`  
**Owner and author:** Ren Solis, People Operations Coordinator (EMP-004)

> Fiction notice: NUCC, its employees, schedules, roles, and contact routes are invented for an educational RAG test corpus. This notice covers administrative scheduling records only. It does not provide clinical staffing or care guidance.

## Notice purpose and effective date

This draft proposes an administrative path for requesting, approving, and recording a change to a published staff schedule at Harbor Point, Alder Creek, or Northgate. The proposed effective date is **2024-09-21**, subject to approval. If approved, this notice would replace NU-OPS-017 for requests handled on or after that date. Until approval is recorded, NU-OPS-005 remains a draft and NU-OPS-017 remains the current approved source if applicable; do not apply the proposed rules below as requirements.

## What counts as a schedule change

The proposed scope is a request to change an already published work date, shift assignment, or site assignment. A request to correct a clerical error would be identified as a correction so the record owner could distinguish it from a requested change.

This notice does not define minimum coverage, qualifications, clinical assignments, or whether a proposed schedule is operationally adequate. It records an administrative request and its disposition; it does not determine clinical or site readiness.

## Approval roles

For coordinator review, this draft proposes that the **site lead for the affected site** approve a schedule change that affects only that site. A request affecting more than one site, a central-office schedule, or the schedule of the site lead would be routed to the **Director of Operations** for a decision. The People Operations Coordinator would maintain the administrative record but would not approve a request solely by receiving or recording it. These approver assignments remain proposals pending reconciliation with NU-OPS-017 and approval of this notice.

Under the proposed rule, a decision would need to be explicit and attributable to the applicable approver role. A submitted request, a copied email, a draft calendar entry, or silence would not constitute approval. If the applicable approver were unavailable, or the request did not fit the proposed cases above, no delegate would be inferred and the request would remain pending while the People Operations Coordinator clarified the route.

## How to submit and record a change

The following intake fields and record workflow are proposed for review, not current requirements:

1. The requester would send the request to the site lead for the affected site. If it spanned sites, concerned a central-office schedule, or affected the site lead’s own schedule, it would go to the Director of Operations through the role-based route in NU-OPS-007.
2. The proposed request fields are affected site or sites, date or dates, the published assignment being changed, the requested change, and the requester’s role. The draft proposes omitting unrelated personal details.
3. The approver would record one of these proposed dispositions: **approved**, **declined**, or **pending**. An approved record would identify the approving role and decision date. A declined record would state the disposition without unnecessary explanation. A pending record would identify the next role responsible for follow-up when known.
4. The proposed recipients of the disposition are the requester and People Operations Coordinator. The Coordinator would update the administrative schedule-change log after receiving an explicit decision and retain the request with its disposition.
5. The proposed handoff would use the recorded disposition. A request alone would not change the published record.

The proposed role-based mailbox for process questions and schedule-change routing is `people-operations@northstar-uc.example.invalid`, matching the draft NU-OPS-007 directory. If this notice is approved, the current directory route for the People Operations Coordinator would be used. The mailbox is fictional corpus data; this draft does not promise response times or establish a substitute approver.

## Same-day change routing

The proposed same-day path is to mark the subject **same-day schedule request** and contact the affected site lead using the route in NU-OPS-007, in addition to sending the written request to the proposed approver. If the case falls under the proposed Director of Operations decision route, that role’s route would also be used. The request would remain pending until an explicit decision was received and entered. This draft does not define an emergency or clinical coverage process.

## Prior-edition status

If approved, NU-OPS-017, *Staff Schedule Change Notice, Prior Edition*, would be superseded for requests handled on or after the approved effective date. Until then, NU-OPS-017 remains the current approved source if applicable, and this draft must not be used as an approval rule. Following approval, the prior notice would remain available only for historical questions about its covered period.

## Questions and contact route

This draft proposes that questions about submission, recording, or an unclassified request go to the People Operations Coordinator at `people-operations@northstar-uc.example.invalid`, with role destinations checked against NU-OPS-007. Neither the contact route nor the Coordinator’s proposed recordkeeping role grants approval authority.
