# NU-OPS-008 — Supply Request and Receiving Guide

**Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC)**  
**Status:** Draft (planned status: current) · **Version:** 1.0  
**Proposed effective date:** 1 October 2024, upon approval · **Review by:** 1 October 2025  
**Applies to:** Harbor Point, Alder Creek, and Northgate · **Access scope:** `site_operations`

> Fictional educational test material. This guide covers ordinary non-clinical office, facilities, and workspace supplies only. It excludes medical, clinical, laboratory, and patient-care materials, and does not provide clinical guidance.

> **Draft notice:** The workflow below is proposed and pending approval by the Director of Operations. It does not become the current procedure unless and until that approval is recorded and the effective date is reached.

## 1. Scope and limits

Use this guide for routine administrative supplies such as paper goods, labels for office use, cleaning and workspace materials, and small facilities items. A site may request an item, but a request is not an approval or a purchase commitment.

Do not use this guide to request or receive medical or patient-care supplies, controlled materials, laboratory items, or any item whose handling is governed by a separate clinical or specialized process. This corpus does not define those processes. Do not substitute an item or create a local handling rule when the applicable process is not established.

## 2. Submit a routine request

The site operations staff member identifies the need and records it in the site supply log. The site lead checks that the request is for an in-scope non-clinical item, confirms the quantity and timing requested, and forwards it to the Facilities Coordinator at `facilities-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid`.

Include:

- Requesting site and date.
- Item description and non-clinical use.
- Quantity requested and unit of measure.
- Current on-site quantity, if known, and requested-by date.
- Reason for request and any relevant existing request or stock reference.
- Site lead review status: submitted, returned for clarification, or forwarded.

The Facilities Coordinator records the request in the central supply queue, checks for a duplicate or missing information, and returns the request to the site when clarification is needed. If central review changes the requested item or quantity, record the change and send it back to the site lead for confirmation before placing the request.

## 3. Approval boundary and status

The Facilities Coordinator coordinates routine supply requests and records their status. This guide does not establish a spending limit, reorder threshold, budget authority, vendor contract, or approval authority for a purchase. A request remains **pending** until the responsible approver is identified and approval is explicitly recorded under an applicable approved process.

Do not treat any of these as approval: a request submission, a receipt acknowledgment, an availability check, an estimated delivery date, or the absence of an objection. If the applicable approver or approval path is not identified, leave the request pending and ask the Director of Operations at `operations-director@northstar-uc.example.invalid` to identify the governing route. Do not infer an approval from role title alone.

Use the following proposed queue states:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Submitted | Sent to the Facilities Coordinator; review is not yet recorded. |
| Clarification needed | A required detail is missing or the request needs site confirmation. |
| Pending approval | The request is awaiting an identified approver’s recorded decision. |
| Approved for fulfillment | An applicable approver’s decision is recorded; fulfillment may proceed within that approval. |
| Ordered / in transit | The order or transfer is recorded, but site receipt is not yet confirmed. |
| Partially received / discrepancy | Some items arrived or a mismatch needs follow-up. |
| Complete | The site confirmed the expected quantity and the request record is closed. |
| Cancelled | The request was withdrawn or not approved; record the reason and decision source when known. |

These states track administration only. They do not guarantee stock availability or delivery timing.

## 4. Routine receiving check

The receiving staff member compares the delivery with the available order or transfer reference before moving items to ordinary site storage. Record the check in the site receiving log.

Confirm and record:

- Site and date/time received.
- Supplier or transfer reference, if shown on the paperwork.
- Item description and quantity expected, received, and visibly discrepant.
- Whether packaging or the delivery record shows a mismatch or visible damage.
- Temporary location and the role taking the next action, if an item is held aside.
- Name or role of the person completing the receipt check, according to the local log convention.

Receiving confirms only what was observed at handoff. It does not approve the purchase, confirm suitability beyond the listed non-clinical request, or certify the contents of an unopened package.

When the order matches and the item is in scope, the receiving staff member records the receipt and hands it to the site lead or designated site operations role for ordinary storage. The site lead updates the supply log with received quantity and closes the request when the request is complete.

## 5. Discrepancies and temporary holding

If quantity, item, condition, or paperwork does not match the order:

1. Record the observed mismatch in the receiving log; distinguish observation from suspected cause.
2. Keep the affected item identifiable and separate from routine stock while its disposition is unresolved. Do not discard, relabel, substitute, or distribute a discrepant item under this guide.
3. Notify the site lead and send the discrepancy summary and record ID to the Facilities Coordinator at `facilities-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid`.
4. The Facilities Coordinator records the follow-up owner and next status when known. The site lead records any verified correction, return, or other disposition.
5. Keep the discrepancy open until the disposition is recorded. Receipt of a message or a partial delivery alone does not close the request.

Do not include unnecessary personal information in the general supply log. If the issue also qualifies for another reporting process, follow that process and cross-reference only the non-sensitive supply request or discrepancy ID where permitted.

## 6. Records and follow-up

Keep the site request and receiving entries linked by a request ID or order reference. The central supply queue records the central status and next owner; the site log records the local need, handoff, and receipt. Updates should show the date, status, role making the update, and source of any confirmed delivery or approval information.

For a request or discrepancy with no listed owner, an unavailable route, or an unresolved routing question, use the after-hours/unavailable-route instructions in NU-OPS-007. The planned route is the Operations Desk Coordinator at `operations-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid` and the relevant central route. Record the attempted route and do not invent an alternate contact or delivery promise. No response-time commitment is established.

## 7. Local exception boundary

NU-OPS-018 is planned as a time-bounded exception for Northgate receiving only. If approved, it will apply only at Northgate and only during the dates and conditions stated in that exception. It does not change routine request approval, receiving, discrepancy, or storage rules at Harbor Point or Alder Creek, or outside the approved exception window. Until NU-OPS-018 is approved and effective, use the approved current receiving process; this draft does not activate an exception.
