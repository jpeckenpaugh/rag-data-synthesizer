# NU-OPS-007 — Site Contact and Escalation Directory

**Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC)**  
**Status:** Draft (planned status: current) · **Version:** 1.0  
**Effective:** 21 September 2024 · **Review by:** 21 March 2025  
**Scope:** All sites · **Access label:** `site_operations`

> Fictional training material. Names, roles, and contact routes in this document are invented for a RAG test corpus. This directory covers administrative and facilities coordination only; it is not a clinical escalation directory.

## How to use this directory

Start with the role responsible for the issue. Use the listed shared mailbox for routine written routing; include the site, a short issue description, when it began, and the record or ticket identifier if one exists. Do not include unnecessary personal or sensitive information in a general operations message.

For a time-sensitive facilities or site interruption, notify the local site lead as well as the central role listed for that topic. The directory gives destinations, not a promise of response time or service restoration. If a route is unavailable, use the after-hours path below and record the attempted contact in the applicable issue record.

## Central operations roles

| Role | Use for | Fictional route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Director of Operations | Organization-wide operational decisions, document ownership questions, and continuity coordination | `operations-director@northstar-uc.example.invalid` |
| Facilities Coordinator | Facilities issue intake, routine supply routing, receiving discrepancies, and vendor coordination | `facilities-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid` |
| People Operations Coordinator | Administrative schedule-change process questions | `people-operations@northstar-uc.example.invalid` |
| Records Coordinator | Administrative records handling or a suspected records misdelivery | `records-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid` |

The shared mailbox names above are fictional routing labels. This directory does not identify individual inbox access, backup coverage, or a response-time commitment.

## Site lead roles

Contact the site lead for local coordination, handoff status, and site-specific records. If the site lead is unavailable, use the central operations route for the issue type; the directory does not establish a named deputy for any site.

| Site | Role | Fictional route |

The Alder Creek route is a fictional role mailbox for corpus testing. It does not identify a registered employee or establish who currently holds that role.

## Topic routing

| Topic | Primary route | Include or record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facilities issue | Facilities Coordinator; copy the local site lead for a time-sensitive site impact | Site, location within site, observed condition, start time if known, priority basis, and issue record ID |
| Routine supply request or receiving discrepancy | Facilities Coordinator; local site lead retains the site receiving record | Item or shipment reference, quantity involved, date received or needed, and discrepancy record ID |
| Schedule-change request or process question | People Operations Coordinator; site lead handles local handoff | Requested date or shift, approval status (requested, approved, or declined), and record location; do not treat a request as approval |
| Administrative records misdelivery | Records Coordinator | Route through the designated records channel; limit details in general email and follow the records procedure |
| Workplace incident report | Quality and Continuity Analyst; notify the site lead for local coordination | Incident record ID, site, date/time, and immediate non-clinical coordination status; use the incident procedure for required details |
| Service interruption | Director of Operations for coordination, with the local site lead; Facilities Coordinator when facilities are involved | Affected site or function, observed status, time last confirmed, actions taken, and next update owner |
| Directory correction or unresolved routing question | Operations Desk Coordinator | Role or route in question, source document if known, and whether a follow-up owner has been assigned |

The applicable procedure controls what information must be recorded and who approves an action. This directory does not grant approval authority.

## After-hours escalation path

For a non-clinical operational issue discovered outside the local team’s normal handoff, send the summary to `operations-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid` and the relevant central route above. Include the site lead route when a site needs local coordination. Record the time and destination used in the relevant issue log. The Operations Desk Coordinator records an unresolved routing question and assigns a follow-up owner when one is known.

This directory does not define an emergency response number, on-call roster, guaranteed monitoring hours, clinical escalation route, or a service-level commitment. If a needed destination is not listed here or in the relevant current procedure, do not infer or invent one; record the gap and ask the Operations Desk Coordinator to confirm it.

## Directory owner and updates

The Operations Desk Coordinator maintains this directory. Send proposed corrections to `operations-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid`. The listed routes remain fictional test data; a correction request does not change a controlled procedure or its approval rules.
