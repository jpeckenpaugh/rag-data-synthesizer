> Fiction notice: NUCC, its employees, schedules, roles, and contact routes are invented for an educational RAG test corpus. This notice covers administrative scheduling records only. It does not provide clinical staffing or care guidance.

## Historical notice and effective period

This document records the prior administrative schedule-change notice for corpus testing. Its effective period began **2024-01-01** and its last effective date was **2024-09-20**. NU-OPS-005 became the successor on **2024-09-21**; that date is not an effective date for this prior edition.

NU-OPS-017 is used only to answer historical questions about its effective period and does not control requests on or after NU-OPS-005’s effective date.

## Prior approval rule

The prior rule was different from the rule in NU-OPS-005:

- For a change affecting one site, the **site lead for that site** reviewed and approved or declined the request.
- For a change affecting more than one site or a central-office schedule, the **People Operations Coordinator** made the approval decision after receiving the affected site leads’ comments.
- A change to the schedule of a site lead was routed to the People Operations Coordinator for decision.

The Director of Operations was not the routine approver under this prior rule. NU-OPS-005 uses a different route for cross-site, central-office, and site-lead schedule changes; that successor rule must not be read backward into this edition.

## Request and recording process

Under the prior process, a staff member sent a written request to the affected site lead, or to the People Operations Coordinator for a multi-site, central-office, or site-lead schedule change. The request identified the site or sites, affected date or dates, published assignment, requested adjustment, and requester role.

The approver returned an explicit **approved** or **declined** decision. A request, copied message, tentative calendar edit, or lack of response did not count as approval. The site lead or People Operations Coordinator receiving the decision forwarded it to the People Operations Coordinator for entry in the schedule-change log. A request remained pending until an explicit decision was recorded. These are historical details, not current requirements.

## Routing and questions

The fictional People Operations role route is `people-operations@northstar-uc.example.invalid`, as listed in NU-OPS-007. The prior process used that route for multi-site and central-office requests, site-lead schedule changes, and questions about recordkeeping. The route is a fictional mailbox label; no individual contact, response time, or substitute approver is established.
