> Fiction notice: NUCC, its employees, schedules, roles, and contact routes are invented for an educational RAG test corpus. This notice covers administrative scheduling records only. It does not provide clinical staffing or care guidance.

## Historical notice and proposed effective period

This document is a draft reconstruction of a prior administrative schedule-change notice for corpus testing. The dates and historical rule below are proposed until coordinator review confirms them. The proposed effective period begins **2024-01-01** and ends immediately before **2024-09-21**, but only if NU-OPS-005 is approved with that effective date.

Until NU-OPS-005 is approved and effective, this draft does not establish a prior approved rule. If NU-OPS-005 remains unapproved, the previously approved source, if any, remains applicable; neither this reconstruction nor the NU-OPS-005 draft should be treated as approved instruction. If both documents are later approved as proposed, NU-OPS-017 would be used only to answer historical questions about its proposed period and would not control requests on or after the replacement’s effective date.

## Proposed prior approval rule

The historical rule proposed for this test fixture is different from the rule proposed in NU-OPS-005:

- For a change affecting one site, the **site lead for that site** would review and approve or decline the request.
- For a change affecting more than one site or a central-office schedule, the **People Operations Coordinator** would make the approval decision after receiving the affected site leads’ comments.
- A change to the schedule of a site lead would be routed to the People Operations Coordinator for decision.

These approver roles are a proposed historical distinction, not an established fact until reconciled by the coordinator. The Director of Operations is not named as the routine approver in this proposed prior rule. NU-OPS-005 proposes a different route for cross-site, central-office, and site-lead schedule changes; that proposed successor rule must not be read backward into this edition.

## Proposed request and recording process

For the proposed prior process, a staff member would send a written request to the affected site lead, or to the People Operations Coordinator for a multi-site, central-office, or site-lead schedule change. The request would identify the site or sites, affected date or dates, published assignment, requested adjustment, and requester role.

The proposed approver would return an explicit **approved** or **declined** decision. A request, copied message, tentative calendar edit, or lack of response would not count as approval. The site lead or People Operations Coordinator receiving the decision would forward it to the People Operations Coordinator for entry in the schedule-change log. A request would remain pending until an explicit decision was recorded. These fields and steps are proposed historical details for review, not current requirements.

## Proposed routing and questions

The fictional People Operations role route is `people-operations@northstar-uc.example.invalid`, as listed in NU-OPS-007. The proposed prior process would use that route for multi-site and central-office requests, site-lead schedule changes, and questions about recordkeeping. The route is a fictional mailbox label; no individual contact, response time, or substitute approver is established.
