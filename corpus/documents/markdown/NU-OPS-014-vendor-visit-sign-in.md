# NU-OPS-014 — Vendor Visit and Contractor Sign-In

**Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC)**  
**Status:** Draft (planned status: current) · **Version:** 1.0  
**Proposed effective date:** 21 September 2024, upon approval · **Review by:** 21 September 2025  
**Applies to:** Harbor Point, Alder Creek, and Northgate · **Access scope:** `site_operations`

> Fictional educational test material. This checklist concerns scheduled, non-clinical facilities work only. It does not authorize access to clinical, patient, laboratory, or restricted work areas and contains no clinical guidance.

> **Draft notice:** This proposed cross-site workflow is pending approval by the Director of Operations. It does not become current unless approval is recorded and the effective date is reached.

This is an internal operations reference for site operations staff and site leads. Staff may communicate the relevant sign-in and approved work-area instructions to a visitor as part of a scheduled visit; contractors are not users of this corpus or intended retrieval audience.

## Before the visit

The Facilities Coordinator coordinates the facilities work request and provides the site lead with the planned visit window, work description, and vendor dispatch or work-order reference. The site lead confirms that the visit is expected and that the work area and scope match the local request. Use NU-OPS-003 for the facilities issue and work-request record; this checklist does not create purchase, contract, or building-access authority.

Before allowing work to begin, the site lead or designated site operations staff member checks the available work request or visit notice for:

- Site and planned date or visit window.
- Non-clinical work description and named work area at a general level.
- Facilities Coordinator or work-order reference.
- Vendor dispatch reference, if provided.
- Site role expected to receive the visitor.

Do not add personal phone numbers, home addresses, identity-document numbers, or other personal details to the site visit log. This corpus does not define identity-verification requirements.

## Arrival and sign-in

At arrival, the receiving site staff member compares the visit with the expected notice and records the visit before work begins. Use the following fields:

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Site and date | Site name and local arrival date |
| Arrival time | Time the visitor arrived at the site |
| Work reference | Facilities issue, work-order, or dispatch reference available to the site |
| Visit organization label | Organization or service label shown on the visit paperwork; do not add unsupported detail |
| Visitor work role | Role or crew function, without personal contact details |
| Site host role | Site role accepting the visit |
| Approved scope | Brief description of the work and general area confirmed for the visit |
| Visit-record status | Expected, arrived, or signed out; this records the visit stage only |
| Work-scope status | Confirmation pending, not authorized to begin, or scope confirmed by the Facilities Coordinator and site lead as described below |

The visit-record status describes whether the visit is expected, has arrived, or has been signed out. It does not verify identity, contract status, or permission to begin work. The separate work-scope status records whether the work reference and local scope have been confirmed; arrival, sign-in, or a prior visit alone does not authorize work. Work may begin only after the Facilities Coordinator confirms the work reference and the site lead confirms the local scope, with the confirming role, time, and reference recorded.

## Scope and work-area boundaries

The site lead confirms the work described matches the visit notice and directs the visitor to the approved non-clinical work area. The visitor may perform only the listed work in that area. A change in task, area, date, or scope requires confirmation from the Facilities Coordinator and site lead before work continues.

This checklist does not establish access to clinical, patient, laboratory, records, staff-only, or other areas outside the confirmed work scope. If the planned work appears to require an area or activity not described in the notice, pause the affected work and ask the Facilities Coordinator to confirm the authorization. Do not infer permission from a prior visit or from the visitor’s statement alone.

## If the visit or authorization cannot be confirmed

If the vendor, work reference, date, scope, or receiving role does not match the available notice:

1. Do not authorize work to begin in the disputed scope or area.
2. Keep the visit-record status **arrived** unless the visitor is later signed out; set the work-scope status to **confirmation pending** and record the mismatch. Avoid personal details.
3. Contact the Facilities Coordinator through `facilities-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid` and notify the site lead. Use NU-OPS-007 for the applicable fictional site route.
4. Resume only after the Facilities Coordinator confirms the work reference and the site lead confirms the local scope. Record the confirming role, time, and reference.
5. If the route is unavailable or the visit is outside the local handoff, use the unavailable-route instructions in NU-OPS-007: contact the Operations Desk Coordinator at `operations-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid` and the relevant central route. Record the attempt; no response time or alternate authorization route is established.

Do not accept an unlisted vendor or changed scope as authorized based solely on an informal message that cannot be linked to a Facilities Coordinator or work reference. This checklist does not define an emergency contractor process.

## Work completion and sign-out

Before the visitor leaves, the site host role records:

- Departure time.
- Work-order or facilities issue reference.
- Work status as reported: completed, partially completed, not started, or follow-up required.
- Work area returned to the site host or left awaiting follow-up, stated factually.
- Any scope difference or discrepancy and the role notified.
- Site role completing the sign-out.

Sign-out records the handoff status; it does not certify technical quality or close a facilities issue. The site lead updates the local visit log and sends any unresolved work or discrepancy to the Facilities Coordinator under NU-OPS-003. Keep the facilities issue open until its disposition is recorded by the responsible owner.

## Record storage

Store the sign-in and sign-out entry with or linked to the site facilities work record, using the site’s controlled operations record location. Retain only the fields needed to establish the visit, scope check, and handoff. Do not copy personal details into general operations notes. If an event also meets another reporting definition, follow that procedure and cross-reference only non-sensitive record IDs where allowed.
