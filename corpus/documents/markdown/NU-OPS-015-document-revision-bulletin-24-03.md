# Document Revision Bulletin 24-03

**NU-OPS-015 · Current**\
Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative · Organization-wide\
Version 1.0 · Effective 2024-03-18 · Access scope: `all_staff`

> Fictional educational test material. This bulletin concerns administrative document routing and identification of current copies at the fictional Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC). It is not guidance for a real organization.

## Status and relationship to NU-OPS-001

This bulletin amends NU-OPS-001, *Operations Governance and Document Control*, effective 2024-03-18. It applies only to the document routing and revision practices stated below.

The bulletin does not replace NU-OPS-001. Where this bulletin addresses a specific routing or current-copy identification step, use that step only after this bulletin is approved and effective. All provisions listed under “Unchanged requirements” remain as written in NU-OPS-001.

## Sections amended

This bulletin amends the following parts of NU-OPS-001 only:

- **Section 3, Required document controls:** adds the routing and repository-entry steps below for a proposed revision and its approved release.
- **Section 5, Identifying and using a controlled copy:** adds the exact repository status and version check below for identifying the current approved copy.

No other section is amended. In particular, this bulletin does not change who may approve a document, the review interval, version-number rules, access labels, temporary-exception requirements, or the handling of superseded and withdrawn copies.

## Routing and release steps

A document owner proposing a change to an organization-wide policy or cross-site procedure will:

1. Prepare the proposed text and a short revision summary that names the document ID, current version, proposed version, affected sections, and reason for change.
2. Route the proposal to the Director of Operations using the role-based route listed in NU-OPS-007. The Director of Operations assigns or confirms any needed review by affected roles; this routing step does not itself approve the change.
3. Keep the proposal marked **Draft** while review or approval is pending. Do not distribute it as an operative requirement.
4. After approval, ensure the controlled repository entry and released document agree on document ID, approved version, effective date, owner, and status before the new copy is presented as current.
5. Mark the replaced copy **Superseded**, record the replacement document ID and effective date, and retain it in the designated archive as required by NU-OPS-001.

For a site-level document that does not change an organization-wide requirement, the owner routes the proposal to the responsible site lead for local review. If the proposed change could conflict with an organization-wide document, route the conflict to the Director of Operations; do not release the local change until the conflict is resolved under NU-OPS-001.

## Current-copy identification

The central operations document repository’s entry is the controlling reference for identifying a current copy. The entry must display the document ID, title, version, status, effective date, owner role, and the date it was last checked or updated.

Treat a copy as current only when all of the following are true:

- The repository entry identifies it as **Current**.
- The released copy’s document ID and version match that entry.
- Its status is approved/current, and its effective date has arrived.
- No later approved version or bulletin in the repository changes the applicable requirement.

If any value is missing, the entry and copy disagree, the effective date has not arrived, or another approved bulletin affects the section, do not decide by file timestamp or filename. Ask the owner or the Director of Operations to confirm the applicable controlled copy. NU-OPS-007 supplies the fictional role-based route; it does not grant approval authority.

Downloaded and printed copies are reference copies. Before relying on one, compare its ID, version, status, and effective date with the repository entry and note the date checked. A copy that cannot be matched is not confirmed as current.

## Document-owner actions

Each document owner should:

- Review the repository entry for documents they own and correct missing or mismatched identifying fields through the approved routing process.
- Use the routing steps above for proposed revisions after the effective date.
- Keep drafts visibly marked **Draft** and separate from repository locations presented as current guidance.
- On release of an approved replacement, mark and archive the prior copy as NU-OPS-001 requires.
- Report an unresolved mismatch to the Director of Operations through the role-based route in NU-OPS-007 rather than choosing a copy by timestamp.

## Unchanged requirements

This bulletin does not change:

- The Director of Operations’ approval of organization-wide operational policies and cross-site procedures, or the limited local approval authority described in NU-OPS-001.
- The requirement that approval be recorded before a draft becomes an approved instruction.
- Required metadata, major/minor version-number logic, or the owner’s duty to describe changes.
- NU-OPS-001’s twelve-month maximum routine review interval or earlier review when circumstances change.
- Requirements for time-bounded, authorized exceptions and the rule that unrelated requirements remain in effect.
- The designated repository/archive model, superseded/withdrawn handling, access-scope labels, or limits on personal information.
- The substantive requirements of any policy, procedure, checklist, or reference document.
