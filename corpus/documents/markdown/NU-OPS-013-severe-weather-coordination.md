# Severe Weather Site Coordination Card

**NU-OPS-013 · Draft for corpus review**  
Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC) · Harbor Point, Alder Creek, and Northgate  
Version 1.0 · Effective 2024-10-01 (proposed) · Access scope: `site_operations`

> Fictional educational test material. This card coordinates administrative site-status reporting only. It does not forecast weather, direct safety actions, provide clinical guidance, or promise when a site will reopen.

## Use this card

Use when a site lead or another staff member eligible for the `site_operations` scope in NU-OPS-010 requests a coordinated status update because reported severe weather may affect site operations. For this corpus, the eligible employee IDs are EMP-001, EMP-002, EMP-003, EMP-006, EMP-007, and EMP-008. Do not use this card to assess a forecast or decide whether anyone should travel, enter, leave, or remain at a site. It does not establish those decisions or instructions.

## Coordination steps

1. **Site lead records the latest known status.** Use the site and update fields below. Mark any unconfirmed detail as “not confirmed”; do not estimate conditions or a reopening time.
2. **Notify central operations.** The site lead owns and sends the update to the Director of Operations for coordination, using the route in NU-OPS-007. If the site lead is unavailable or the owner is unknown, mark the owner as unconfirmed and use the applicable role-based route in NU-OPS-007 to request coordination; do not infer or assign a substitute owner. Record the local site lead as notified only when that notification is confirmed.
3. **Share a staff update.** The site lead shares confirmed administrative status with affected site operations staff through the site’s approved internal channel. The corpus does not name a messaging platform or establish a staff notification list; confirm the channel locally rather than assuming one.
4. **Update on change and handoff.** The proposed default is for the site lead to send an update when a material site-status fact changes and at each shift handoff while coordination remains active. Record the site lead as owner only when confirmed. If the owner is unavailable or unknown, mark it unconfirmed and use the applicable route in NU-OPS-007 to request coordination; do not infer a substitute. No fixed timed cadence is established by this card.
5. **Hand off service interruptions.** If the update concerns an operational service interruption, coordinate under NU-OPS-006. This card does not replace that procedure or establish its detailed steps.

For an issue discovered outside the local team’s normal handoff, NU-OPS-007 lists the Operations Desk Coordinator and relevant central role routes. It does not establish guaranteed monitoring hours, an on-call roster, or a response-time commitment.

## Site status update fields

Use one short entry per update. These proposed status labels are for consistent reporting, not a safety classification:

| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Site | Harbor Point, Alder Creek, or Northgate |
| Updated at | Date and local time of this report |
| Reported by | Role, not unnecessary personal details |
| Status | `Operating as last reported`, `Operational impact reported`, `Unavailable for confirmation`, or `Not confirmed` |
| Basis and last confirmed | What was directly confirmed, by which role/source, and when; distinguish reports from direct confirmation |
| Affected function | Administrative or facility function affected, if known; otherwise “not confirmed” |
| Coordination owner | Site lead, if confirmed; otherwise “unconfirmed” until resolved through a role-based route in NU-OPS-007 |
| Next update | Event that will trigger the next update (material change or shift handoff); do not enter a predicted restoration or reopening time |

Do not include weather predictions, personal travel details, patient information, clinical status, or unsupported claims about site safety or service availability. If a field is unknown, label it unknown rather than filling it by inference.

## Central and role-based routes

For service interruption coordination, NU-OPS-007 lists the Director of Operations with the local site lead, and the Facilities Coordinator when facilities are involved. Use those role-based routes as applicable and follow NU-OPS-006 for the interruption process. Do not invent a weather desk, public alert route, emergency contact, or deputy not listed in the current directory.

## Limits and review

This card records administrative status and update ownership. It does not decide whether a site is safe, open, closed, or ready to resume operations. A status is time-specific and must not be treated as a forecast or guarantee. Questions not answered by this card or the related current procedure remain unresolved until an authorized source establishes the answer.

The Quality and Continuity Analyst owns this card. The proposed review date is six months after its effective date; revise sooner if related coordination roles or procedures change.
