# NU-OPS-018 — Northgate Supply Receiving Exception

**Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC)**  
**Status:** Draft; inactive pending approval · **Version:** 1.0  
**Proposed window:** 10 October 2024 through 24 October 2024, inclusive  
**Site:** Northgate only · **Access scope:** `operations_leads`  
**Owner:** Northgate Site Lead

> Fictional educational test material. This proposed exception covers administrative supply receipt handling only. It is not active unless NU-OPS-008 and this exception are both approved and effective. It does not provide clinical or patient-care guidance.

## 1. Exception purpose and owner

This draft proposes a short-term receiving arrangement for Northgate while the site's receiving desk is proposed to be unavailable during a planned records-room reconfiguration. All details of this condition and arrangement are fictional proposals for corpus review.

The proposed exception addresses only a delivery of eligible supplies arriving without its order or transfer reference available at handoff. If approved, the Northgate Site Lead owns the local receiving record and temporary hold. The Facilities Coordinator remains the central supply-routing role under NU-OPS-008.

## 2. Site, supply, and date scope

If approved, this exception would apply only:

- At Northgate.
- From 10 October through 24 October 2024, inclusive.
- To non-clinical paper goods and office-use labels covered by NU-OPS-008.
- When the delivery's order or transfer reference is unavailable to the receiving staff member at handoff.

It would not cover other supply classes, other sites, or deliveries outside those dates. Medical, clinical, laboratory, patient-care, controlled, or otherwise separately governed materials remain outside scope and must not be handled under this exception.

## 3. Proposed temporary receiving steps

If both this notice and NU-OPS-008 have been approved and are effective, Northgate receiving staff may use these steps for an in-scope delivery lacking its reference at handoff:

1. Check the visible item description, count, and package condition. Do not open a sealed package solely to identify its contents.
2. Make a provisional entry in the Northgate receiving log with date and time, item description, observed quantity, visible condition, carrier or supplier reference if shown, and the receiving staff member's role.
3. Label the entry **reference pending** and identify the next owner as the Northgate Site Lead. Do not record the delivery as matched, complete, or approved.
4. The Northgate Site Lead must designate and record an appropriate non-clinical administrative holding location before this step is used. Keep the package intact there, separate from ordinary stock, until the order or transfer reference is matched. This is a proposed local arrangement, not a location established by NU-OPS-008. If no appropriate location is confirmed and recorded, this exception cannot be used for that delivery; follow the applicable approved current process.
5. Send the provisional entry ID and available delivery details to the Facilities Coordinator. The site lead updates the local record when the reference is confirmed and applies the ordinary receiving steps in NU-OPS-008 before moving eligible items to ordinary storage.

This is a receipt-and-hold path only. It does not authorize substitution, distribution, use, disposal, or a purchase commitment while the reference remains unconfirmed.

## 4. Recordkeeping and discrepancy handling

The Northgate Site Lead owns the provisional local receiving entry and records the later reference match or the unresolved disposition. The Facilities Coordinator remains the central follow-up route. Keep the entry open until the reference is matched or the discrepancy is handled under NU-OPS-008 and its disposition is recorded.

If the item, quantity, condition, or paperwork also presents a discrepancy under NU-OPS-008, follow that guide's discrepancy requirements, including keeping the affected item identifiable and separate and routing the record to the Facilities Coordinator. This exception does not relax or replace those requirements.

## 5. Unchanged request and approval rules

This proposed exception changes no supply request, purchase, or fulfillment approval rule. A receipt acknowledgment or provisional log entry is not approval. NU-OPS-008's request and approval boundaries remain in force, as do its routine discrepancy, record, and closure requirements except for the narrowly stated temporary reference-pending receipt path above.

All requirements unrelated to this specific Northgate receipt condition remain unchanged. Harbor Point and Alder Creek continue under their applicable current receiving process.

## 6. Expiration and return to routine process

The proposed exception expires at the end of 24 October 2024. Beginning 25 October 2024, Northgate would return to the routine receiving process in NU-OPS-008. Any provisional record still open at expiration remains subject to the ordinary discrepancy and follow-up rules; expiration does not close it or establish a disposition.

If either NU-OPS-008 or NU-OPS-018 is not approved and effective for the proposed window, this exception is inactive and staff must use the applicable approved current process. This draft alone does not authorize the temporary steps above.
