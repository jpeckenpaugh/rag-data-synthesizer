# Workplace Incident Reporting Guide

**NU-OPS-011 · Draft for corpus review**  
Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC) · All sites  
Version 1.0 · Effective 2024-10-01 (proposed) · Access scope: `site_operations`

> Fictional educational test material. This guide covers administrative reporting for non-clinical workplace incidents. It is not clinical, emergency-response, legal, or safety advice.

## 1. Scope and non-clinical boundary

Use this guide to document and route a workplace incident for administrative follow-up at Harbor Point, Alder Creek, or Northgate. For this guide, a reportable incident is an unplanned workplace event or near miss that may require a facilities, workplace coordination, or administrative follow-up record.

This guide does not direct an immediate response, assess injuries, recommend treatment, or establish clinical or emergency procedures. It does not establish how to handle patient-care events or records. Any immediate response or separate reporting process is outside this document; this guide begins with administrative reporting and must not be used to infer those instructions.

If the same event also meets NU-OPS-003’s definition of a facilities issue, create both the workplace incident record and the facilities issue record. Cross-reference only the non-sensitive incident and ticket IDs; do not copy sensitive details from one record into the other.

## 2. Roles and reporting route

- **Reporter:** sends a concise administrative report with the known facts and identifies anything still unknown. Do not delay a report to fill fields that are not known.
- **Site lead:** receives notice for local coordination and confirms who will own any site-level administrative follow-up.
- **Quality and Continuity Analyst:** receives the workplace incident report and coordinates the reporting process and follow-up record.

Send the report to the Quality and Continuity Analyst at `quality-continuity@northstar-uc.example.invalid`. Notify the local site lead for local coordination. These are fictional role-based routes listed in the draft NU-OPS-007 directory, which is planned to be the current directory after approval. The directory does not promise a response time or identify a deputy. This guide does not create an additional after-hours or emergency route.

## 3. Minimum administrative incident record

Record only information needed to identify the event and coordinate follow-up. The proposed minimum fields are:

| Field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Incident reference | The reference assigned in the site’s designated administrative incident log; if no reference is available, state “not assigned.” |
| Date and time | When the event occurred, or the best known range; mark estimates as estimates. |
| Site and general location | Harbor Point, Alder Creek, or Northgate, plus a non-sensitive area description. |
| Category | A short administrative category, such as facilities condition, workplace interaction, property issue, or near miss. |
| Factual summary | Brief observable facts; separate direct observation from what was reported by someone else. |
| Administrative coordination | Whether the site lead was notified, when, and any non-clinical coordination status known. Do not record or characterize treatment or clinical status. |
| Follow-up | The assigned role, if known; the next administrative action; and whether it is open, pending, or complete. |

Do not include patient identifiers, clinical details, diagnoses, treatment information, speculation about fault, or unnecessary personal details in this administrative report. Do not attach patient or clinical records. If an incident cannot be summarized without such information, stop at a non-identifying description and ask the Records Coordinator how an administrative report should be routed; NU-OPS-009 governs records handling and does not turn this guide into a clinical reporting process.

## 4. Administrative reporting workflow

1. Prepare a brief report using the minimum fields above. Mark unknown details as unknown rather than filling gaps by inference.
2. Send it to the Quality and Continuity Analyst using the route in NU-OPS-007. Notify the site lead when local coordination is needed.
3. Record the date, time, and role-based destination used in the designated administrative incident log. If no log or reference has been assigned, note that the record location is unconfirmed and ask the site lead or Quality and Continuity Analyst to identify the approved location.
4. The site lead or Quality and Continuity Analyst identifies an administrative follow-up owner when one is established. Record the owner by role and the follow-up status; do not mark an item complete until its administrative action is confirmed.
5. The Quality and Continuity Analyst reviews the report for missing administrative fields and records whether the report is accepted for follow-up or needs clarification. A request for clarification does not change the event facts.

This workflow does not set a response deadline, severity scale, retention period, investigative standard, or software platform. None is established by this guide; do not infer one from the reporting route.

## 5. Access and records

The front matter assigns this document the `site_operations` access label. NU-OPS-010 defines that label and its fictional employee-ID eligibility for corpus retrieval. Apply both access scope and site scope when retrieving related records. This label is a test convention, not a real authorization rule.

Keep the incident report in the designated administrative location once that location is confirmed. Access, retention, and handling of other organizational records remain subject to their applicable documents, including NU-OPS-009 for records handling. This guide does not establish a retention schedule or expand access to a separate record.

## 6. Unresolved or out-of-scope issues

If the applicable reporting location, record reference, or follow-up owner is not established, mark it as unknown and route the question to the Quality and Continuity Analyst. NU-OPS-007 lists the role-based route but does not establish a named deputy or guaranteed monitoring hours. Do not invent a destination, deadline, or outcome.

Questions about immediate response, clinical reporting, or patient records are outside this guide. This corpus document does not establish an answer to those questions.
