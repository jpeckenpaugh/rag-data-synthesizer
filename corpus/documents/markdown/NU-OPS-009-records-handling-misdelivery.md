# Records Handling and Misdelivery Procedure

**Northstar Urgent Care Cooperative (NUCC)**  
**Document ID:** NU-OPS-009 · **Status:** Draft (planned status: current) · **Version:** 1.0  
**Proposed issue date:** 2024-09-10 · **Proposed effective date:** 2024-09-21 · **Proposed review date:** 2025-09-21  
**Site scope:** Organization-wide · **Access scope:** `operations_leads`  
**Owner and author:** Dev Arlen, Records Coordinator (EMP-005)

> Fiction notice: NUCC, its employees, records, and routes are invented for an educational RAG test corpus. This proposed procedure concerns administrative organizational records only. It excludes patient, clinical, and care records and is not legal, privacy, or security guidance.

> **Draft status:** This cross-site procedure is proposed for review. Under NU-OPS-001, it would require Director of Operations approval before becoming a current requirement. Do not treat the steps below as approved instructions until approval and an effective date are recorded.

## Scope and handling principles

This proposed procedure covers routine internal routing of non-clinical organizational records and the administrative response when such a record is sent to an unintended internal or external destination. Examples include facilities work records, supply receipts, schedule-change records, vendor visit forms, and operations correspondence.

It does not cover patient or clinical records, clinical information, external legal requests, or records whose handling rules are not established in this corpus. Do not copy, quote, or add the contents of a misdelivered record to a general issue log. If a question falls outside the scope above, pause this process and ask the Records Coordinator to identify the appropriate owner; this draft does not define that other process.

The draft proposes three handling principles:

- Use the role-based destination in NU-OPS-007 rather than guessing an individual recipient.
- Include only the minimum information needed to identify and route the issue.
- Keep the record’s contents out of broad messages and reports unless an approved, need-to-know route is established.

These are proposed corpus conventions, not claims about real records-management requirements.

## Internal records routing

For routine routing, the proposed process is to send the record to the role or site destination named by the procedure that created it. If that procedure does not identify a destination, use the Records Coordinator route in NU-OPS-007: `records-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid`. Do not infer that a recipient is authorized merely because they were copied on an earlier message.

For a record intended for a site, identify the site in the routing note and use the applicable role route in NU-OPS-007. If the destination is unclear or the record appears to contain material outside this procedure’s scope, do not forward it broadly; contact the Records Coordinator for direction. NU-OPS-007 supplies fictional destinations but does not independently grant access to a record.

## Recognizing a misdelivery

For this proposed procedure, a misdelivery is a non-clinical organizational record sent to a person, shared mailbox, site, or distribution list that was not its intended destination. Examples include an incorrect site name, a stale role mailbox, an unintended recipient on a message, or a physical envelope delivered to the wrong site office.

Do not decide that a delivery is authorized based only on a familiar name or role. If the intended destination cannot be confirmed from the originating procedure or record owner, treat the route as unconfirmed and ask the Records Coordinator.

## Proposed containment and notification

If a misdelivery is suspected, this draft proposes the following sequence:

1. **Stop further distribution.** Do not forward, reply-all, reproduce, or summarize the record’s contents to additional recipients.
2. **Limit access where practical.** For an electronic item, stop opening or sharing it and use the available administrative controls to restrict further circulation. For a physical item, keep it closed and place it aside for a controlled handoff. This document does not specify technical or physical security controls.
3. **Notify the Records Coordinator.** Use `records-desk@northstar-uc.example.invalid` from NU-OPS-007. Include the site, approximate date and time noticed, record type or neutral reference, intended destination if known, and the action already taken. Do not attach or quote the record unless the Coordinator requests it through an approved route.
4. **Notify a site lead when local coordination is needed.** Use the role route in NU-OPS-007 and share only the minimum status needed to prevent further routing. Do not send the record contents to the site lead by default.
5. **Wait for routing direction.** Do not independently resend the record or delete the source copy. The Records Coordinator would identify the next owner or route under the proposed process.

If the Records Coordinator route is unavailable, the proposed fallback is to contact the Operations Desk Coordinator through NU-OPS-007 and ask that the routing question be recorded. This fallback is a proposal and does not authorize the Desk Coordinator to inspect the record contents or make a substantive access decision.

## Minimum incident record and follow-up

The proposed misdelivery record contains only:

- A short tracking identifier, if one has been assigned.
- Site and date/time the issue was noticed.
- Record category or neutral reference, without its substantive contents.
- Intended destination, if known, and the route where it was found.
- Containment steps taken and the role notified.
- Current status: **open**, **awaiting direction**, or **closed**.
- Follow-up owner by role and next check date, when assigned.

Do not place names of unintended recipients, personal details, record contents, or speculative explanations in a general log unless the Records Coordinator establishes a specific need and approved location. The proposed owner of the follow-up record is the Records Coordinator. The Coordinator would confirm the intended route with the originating record owner, record the disposition, and close the item when the routing issue is resolved. If ownership or resolution is not established, retain the status as open and name the next role responsible when known; do not infer closure from silence.

## Access limits and escalation

This document is labeled `operations_leads` under the corpus conventions in NU-OPS-010. The label limits retrieval in the fictional test corpus; it is not an authorization decision for a real person or system. Apply the document’s exact scope and site metadata. If caller eligibility is unknown, do not disclose its contents on the assumption that the caller is authorized.

The Records Coordinator is the proposed first route for administrative misdelivery questions. The Director of Operations is the proposed escalation owner when the issue spans sites, the route remains disputed, or a procedure conflict affects more than one team. Use the roles and fictional destinations in NU-OPS-007. This procedure does not establish a response time, external reporting duty, or legal conclusion.

## Out-of-scope requests and unresolved cases

If asked to locate or disclose a patient record, clinical document, employee’s personal file, or another category not covered here, state that this procedure does not establish the requested access or handling rule. Do not invent a destination or retrieve content from a broader source based only on a similar title. Route the question to the Records Coordinator for identification of an applicable process; the answer may remain unresolved in this corpus.
