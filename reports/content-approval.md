## Ratified conventions

- Issue, effective, review, working-record, supersession, precedence, and exception-window dates are established as fictional corpus history.
- NU-OPS-006 uses 60-minute updates during active service interruptions and faster updates on material change. NU-OPS-013 retains its separate severe-weather triggers and material-change/shift-handoff convention without a fixed cadence.
- NU-OPS-019's approved workflow assigns site-level submissions to site leads; facilities and supply-record eligibility checks to the Facilities Coordinator; interruption-list confirmation to the Director of Operations; and deduplication, compilation, and missing-source flags to the Quality and Continuity Analyst.

## Deliberate unknowns retained

The corpus does not establish the controlled-document repository location; purchase authority or a spending threshold; site handoff-record locations or retention periods; vendor visit-log retention period; Alder Creek Site Lead backup; or the platform, underlying record repository, or actual values for monthly metrics. Documents preserve these unknowns explicitly. They are intentional abstention cases for RAG evaluation.
